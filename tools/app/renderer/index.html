Treble listening test
Personal treble for the selected measurement, above ~6 kHz.
You hear one treble band on its own, then pink noise with that band nudged up or down, and say whether it sticks out.
You hear two short bursts of noise, each one treble band, and say whether the second is quieter, louder or the same. Each band is matched against its neighbours.
Before you start: cable as system output, normal listening volume, quiet room. If you EQ from a measurement, fit and write it up to ~6 kHz first.
Afterwards: Apply to EQ puts the result straight into the EQ's flat bands, or save it as a profile for the TREBLE view and whole-target auto-fit.
