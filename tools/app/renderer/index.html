Treble listening test
Personal treble for the selected measurement, above ~6 kHz.
Pink noise plays through the cable. Each round you hear one treble band on its own, then the full noise with that band nudged up or down, and you say whether it sticks out. The result is a per-tip profile of the peaks and dips your ear hears, used in place of the rig's treble detail.
Before you start: fit and write your EQ up to ~6 kHz, leave the treble alone, set the cable as your system output, and turn the volume to a normal listening level in a quiet room.
